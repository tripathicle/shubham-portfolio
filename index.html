About

Engineer who turns chaos into clarity.

DevOps & Cloud Engineer with 5+ years of experience designing, automating, and securing cloud infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines at scale. I've delivered enterprise-grade platforms across automotive, email marketing, and e-commerce domains — working with both monolithic and microservices architectures.

My work spans Hub-and-Spoke Terraform architecture on Azure — modular landing zones with 150+ resources, peered VNets, centralized networking, and policy-driven governance. I build DevSecOps pipelines with GitHub Actions and Jenkins, hardened by TFSec, Checkov, TFLint, and TruffleHog.

Currently building AI infrastructure — a full code-first journey through Agent Development Kit and OpenAI SDK — deploying, scaling, and securing agent-driven workloads across Gemini, Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, Llama, Qwen, and open-source models.

Everything I build is designed to be cloned, run, pulled apart, and adapted — across Azure, GitHub Actions, Azure Pipelines, Docker, Kubernetes, and modern AI tooling.
